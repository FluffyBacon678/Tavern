## Performance

The bug hunt's frame times were inflated: nine test runs were going at once. Measured alone afterwards, then fixed:

| Case | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Full house, windowed, 5× (median / p95 / worst) | 17.0 / 28.4 / 41.2 ms | 16.7 / 17.8 / 18.8 ms |
| Small tavern, windowed, 5× | 16.7 / 19.3 ms | unchanged (vsync-locked) |
| Biggest tavern, headless, speed reached of 5× asked | 2.7× (last commit) | 4.4× |
| Biggest tavern, windowed | 52.4 / 115.6 ms, 4.3× | 20.5 / 54.4 ms, 4.8× |

What changed:

- **HUD:**
  - The four-times-a-second refresh is split over three frames (figures, checklist, trouble line). Together they pushed a vsync frame past 16.7 ms, so one frame in fifteen showed for two.
  - The trouble line's "ingredients in use" is kept until the grid changes.
- **Job scan:**
  - Benches, shelves, counters, tables and bars are cached until `BuildGrid.revision` moves.
  - Each good's stacks and each tile's benches are indexed once per scan.
  - Product stock comes from the scan's own count.
- **Guests' service pass:** counters, bars and tills are cached the same way.
- **`ItemWorld.tiles_with`:** sorts packed keys natively; equally near stacks now come in put-down order. The tutorial, soak and level results came out identical.

The biggest tavern is still short of the 2026-09-28 figure (16.8 ms median at full 5×). The workers' job search (~55 ms per game-second for 117 staff) is the next cost.
